FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

How do I apply to placement drives?
After registering as a student and completing your profile, browse active drives on your dashboard. Each drive shows eligibility, CTC, and deadline. Click "Apply" — your profile and resume are auto-attached. You'll receive a confirmation notification instantly.
How does the chat system work?
A chat room is automatically created when a recruiter shortlists you. By default, you communicate through categorized query buttons (Schedule, Links, Documents, Status). The recruiter can unlock free-text for 5 minutes when detailed communication is needed. Admins can observe chats in read-only mode for moderation.
How do recruiters get verified?
When a recruiter registers, they enter the admin's verification queue. Admins review their company details, email domain, and documents, then approve or reject with remarks. Only verified recruiters can post placement drives. This prevents fraudulent postings.
Can I create an admin account?
No. The master admin is created via the Django createsuperuser command. Subsequent admins are promoted internally through the admin dashboard. There is no "Admin" option on the public registration page. This is a security measure to prevent unauthorized access.
What happens after I'm selected?
Your application status changes to "Selected" and your profile earns a "Placed" badge. You can view your complete offer details and placement history from your dashboard. The placement coordinator is also notified for institutional records.
Is my data secure?
All data is stored securely with Django's authentication system. Passwords are hashed, sensitive routes enforce POST requests, and the chat system uses safe update patterns (filter().update() instead of model.save()) to prevent recursion bugs. Admin observer mode ensures chat moderation without compromising privacy.
