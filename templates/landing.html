How it works

Go from profile
to offer in 4 steps

Everything from registration to offer letter — handled seamlessly by Campus Connect.

1

Create your profile

Register as a student, fill in your academics, upload your resume and add skills. Your profile is your digital resume for every recruiter.

2

Browse & apply to drives

Explore placement drives filtered to your eligibility. One-click apply — the portal verifies your CGPA and branch automatically.

3

Track your applications

See real-time status updates — Applied, Shortlisted, or Selected — and get notified by email and portal alerts instantly.

4

Attend interviews & get placed

View your interview schedule, location, and meeting links all in one place. Land your offer and celebrate your placement!

Features

Everything a campus
portal should be

Built for students, recruiters, and administrators — with every workflow covered out of the box.

Auto Eligibility Check

The portal verifies your CGPA and branch against each drive's criteria before letting you apply — no surprises.

Smart Notifications

Email + portal alerts for every milestone — application received, shortlisted, interview scheduled, offer made.

Interview Scheduling

Admins can schedule interviews with date, time, venue, and meeting link. Students see everything in one screen.

Rich Student Profiles

Build a complete profile with resume PDF upload, profile photo, LinkedIn + GitHub links, skills, certifications, CGPA, and a live profile completion indicator. Recruiters see everything they need at a glance.

Resume Upload Profile Photo Skills Certifications LinkedIn GitHub Completion Score

OAuth Login

Google and GitHub login with role-based access — students, recruiters, and admins each see a different portal.

Search & Filter

Filter drives by company name, role, branch, or job type. Find the right opportunity in seconds.

Admin Command Center

The admin dashboard gives a bird's-eye view of all students, recruiters, applications, and placements. Update statuses, schedule interviews, and manage the entire placement cycle from one screen.

Placement Stats Student Management Status Updates Interview Scheduling Drive Management

Application Tracker

Students can view and withdraw applications. Status flows from Applied → Shortlisted → Selected or Rejected.
